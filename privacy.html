Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Short version: TAPE does not collect any data. Everything you record and edit is processed on your iPhone and stays there unless you decide to share it.

What TAPE does on your device

  • Camera and microphone are used only while you use the camera, to record video with sound. Frames are processed on your iPhone's GPU in real time.
  • Photos: TAPE saves your recordings and exports to your photo library when you ask it to (it only needs permission to add photos). Videos you import are chosen by you in the system picker; TAPE sees only those.
  • Music files you add in the editor are picked by you in the system file picker and stay in the app.
  • Your tapes and projects are stored inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What TAPE does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No uploads: TAPE has no server. Your videos never leave your device through TAPE.

Sharing

When you tap Share to Instagram Reels, TAPE hands the finished video to the Instagram app on your iPhone using Meta's official sharing feature. From then on, Instagram's own privacy policy applies. The same is true for any app you choose in the system share sheet.

Purchases

If TAPE offers paid features, payments are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. TAPE never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, TAPE can remind you one day before it ends with a local notification, scheduled on your device. It is only shown if you allow notifications.

Children

TAPE collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
