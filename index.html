TAPE

Your iPhone is a camcorder now.

Twelve camcorders from 1987 to 2007, simulated in real time: tape noise, colour bleed, dropped frames and the date burned into the picture. Then cut it, add text and music, and post it.

Frequently asked

Where are my recordings?

In the library (bottom left in the camera). With Save to Photos on, every recording is also saved to your photo library.

Where are my edits?

Library → Projects. Edits are saved automatically and reopen exactly as you left them.

Why is there no sound?

Check that TAPE may use the microphone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

How do I add music from Instagram?

Export your tape, then tap Share to Instagram Reels. Instagram opens with your video, and you can pick any song from its library there.

Does TAPE upload my videos?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
